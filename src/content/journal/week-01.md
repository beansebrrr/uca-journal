## Generating our own Poster

Among the activities handed out to us, we have a few standout ones that provide actual tangible outputs. One of which is a poster featuring randomly generated colors and a randomly generated assortment of superhero names.

![Week 1 Poster](@assets/images/week-1-poster.png)

Actually, the poster's text could feature any kind of topic we want; but we decided to go for superhero names for the fun of it, and also because we already brought the topic up at an earlier conversation.

The hero names originate from list of predefined words containing a prefix, title, and suffix. These combinations are then combined randomly to generate a whole new name.

What caught me off guard while testing this generator was that I still had to keep grammar and coherence in mind, so that the names don't sound unnatural (e.g. you would definitely want it to say "strongest hero in the world" instead of "in the world strongest hero")

The colors, on the other hand, are generated by randomly generating 3 values between `0` and `255`; representing the intensity of red, green, and blue in the color. These numbers are then put together so that they form a <abbr title="Hexadecimal">hex</abbr> code. These hex codes can be read by `matplotlib` to then generate the color and display it onto the poster.

Deciding on the colors, we wanted to have strong reds and blues, imitating the colors of a lot of other superheroes. But with those reds and blues mixing, we ended up with a lot of magenta, which is honestly a bit more fun.

## Moods

There was an extra activity which we decided to take on: a mood-driven generator. We generate posters like earlier; but this time, the colors and the words displayed onto the canvas depends on the mood we type onto the terminal.

We began by defining some more moods like `silly` and `angry`, so that it won't just be the already-provided `calm`. Each mood contains its own set of words, and parameters of what colors will be generated. `silly` has lots of yellow and greens, while `angry` contains a lot of reds and oranges.
